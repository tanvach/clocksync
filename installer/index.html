1 · Board

2 · Install

Connect the board with a USB data cable, then:

This browser can't reach USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. The installer only works on an https page.

Updating? Leave “Erase device” unticked to keep Wi-Fi and settings.

Board not detected? Hold its BOOT button while plugging it in, then try again.

3 · Set up

From your phone, join the Wi-Fi network clocksync-setup. In the page that opens, pick your network and your clock's station. After that the clock is at clocksync.local.
